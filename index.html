Our Method

The Lean-First Method

Every engagement follows the same four steps, whatever the technology. It is how we avoid the two most common consulting failures: solving the wrong problem, and building more than the problem needs.

  1. 1

    Diagnose

    We establish the business problem and measure the current state before proposing anything.

  2. 2

    Simplify

    We look for the smallest set of parts that solves the problem. More tools rarely means better results.

  3. 3

    Prove

    Every change is measured before and after, so success is a number and not an opinion.

  4. 4

    Hand over

    Documentation and a support window mean you are never dependent on us.
