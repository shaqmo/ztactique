What's Included

Control You Can Show, Not Just Claim

Governance that is short enough to follow and specific enough to audit. It works for AI systems we built and for ones you bought.

AI Policy & Risk

  • An inventory of where AI is already used in your organization
  • A short, usable AI policy that staff will actually follow
  • Risk classification by use case, with named owners

Evaluation & Testing

  • Test sets built from your real tasks and edge cases
  • Quality, accuracy, and safety measured before launch
  • Regression checks whenever a model or prompt changes

Guardrails & Human Oversight

  • Approval steps for sensitive or irreversible actions
  • Access limited to the data and tools each system needs
  • Clear escalation to a person when the system is unsure

Monitoring & Audit

  • Logging of inputs, outputs, and decisions
  • Error, drift, and cost monitoring with alert thresholds
  • An audit trail ready for internal review or an external auditor
Regulatory Readiness

Know Which Rules Apply to You

AI regulation is arriving unevenly across regions. We map your use cases against the rules that apply to you and flag what needs action.

  • Data-protection law
  • EU AI Act, where relevant
  • Sector rules
  • Customer and procurement requirements

We provide technical and operational readiness work. We do not provide legal advice.
